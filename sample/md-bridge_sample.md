## Executive Summary

Meridian Logistics is relocating its **regional headquarters** to the Riverside Business Park. This proposal outlines scope, timeline, and budget for a *turnkey* fit-out of approximately **2,400 m²** across three floors.

The project prioritizes:

## Scope of Work

### 1. Space Planning

Our team will deliver a revised layout based on Meridian's ***hybrid-first*** work model. Two workshops with department leads are included in Phase 1.

Deliverables include:

1. Test-fit drawings (PDF + DWG)
2. Furniture schedule with lead times
3. Signage and wayfinding concept

### 2. Build & MEP

**Structural:** Minor partition adjustments only; no load-bearing changes.

**MEP:** Upgrade HVAC zones on Level 2, add **18** additional data drops, and install occupancy sensors tied to the existing BMS.

**Finishes:** Carpet tile (Interface *Composure* range), acoustic ceiling baffles in open areas, and painted plasterboard elsewhere.

### 3. Furniture & Equipment

We propose a mix of sit-stand desks, phone booths, and a ***24-seat*** training room. All loose furniture carries a **5-year** manufacturer warranty.

## Assumptions & Exclusions

The following are ***explicitly excluded*** from this estimate:

- Landlord shell-and-core works
- IT network configuration beyond patch panels
- Artwork and planting (optional add-on)

Assumptions:

- Site access Monday–Friday, 07:00–18:00
- Meridian provides a single point of contact for sign-offs
- No asbestos-containing materials are present (per landlord disclosure)
